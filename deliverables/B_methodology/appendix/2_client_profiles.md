# Appendix 2: Client profiles

> As of 4 November 2026. All people and records are fictional.

## Conventions

- All ten clients use the same fields. Unknown information is marked "not recorded"; "no purchase" and similar mean confirmed absence. The two are never mixed.
- Purchases are the client's complete record at LACAZE and match the store data. Product, date, colour and price are store-system data; purpose and feedback are background for writing cases and are not in the store system.
- Cumulative spend and tier are computed from purchases, never entered by hand.
- Needs and preferences record only what the client has said; nothing is inferred from age, occupation or tier.
- Profiles are background for writing cases, not input to the tool. The tool receives only the client ID and the conversation; preferences and habits come through in the conversation.
- Each case's conversation is independent; the "last interaction" in a given case is whatever its conversation shows.
