# Appendix 1: Brand and store data

LACAZE, its clients, products, events and policies are fictional. They follow the business logic of luxury leather goods in mainland China.

## The brand

| | |
|---|---|
| Name | LACAZE |
| Founded | Paris, 1923 |
| Origin | A workshop for travel leather goods and saddlery, later known for handbags |
| Category | Handbags only |
| Tone | Restrained, classic, understated; craft and heritage first. No discounts, no price comparisons, no promotions |
| One line | Quiet Parisian craft, for those who know |

## Clients

Mostly women (about three in four), aged 30 to 55: business owners and executives, finance, legal and medical professionals, and family-wealth clients. Men usually buy as gifts or for their own work bags. Clients look for recognition and an experience that cannot be copied, not value for money, and dislike being treated as a sale. The advisor's personal relationship with the client is the core asset.

## How advisors speak

**Tone**: formal but natural, respectful, warm and restrained; specific rather than effusive. Invitations are gentle ("为您预留", "期待与您相约"), never "buy now". Clients are addressed as Mr / Ms plus surname; a long-standing client may be called by a preferred name, but never with e-commerce terms.

**Red lines**:
- E-commerce forms of address such as "亲", "宝贝", "亲爱的"
- Offering discounts, comparing prices, or pushing for a quick sale
- Exclamation marks piled up, or false urgency ("今晚截止")
- Prying into income, marriage, home address or anything unrelated to the service
- Advisors boasting about themselves
- Empty promises ("我帮您催一下" with no real action)
- Generic greetings that could be sent to anyone

**Encouraged**:
- Taking personal ownership ("I will look after you")
- Turning what is known about the client into a well-timed service, such as a care reminder or an event invitation
- Answering price objections with value, craft and existing store services; never negotiating, never promising allocation or delivery dates

## Store services

These apply only to bags bought at official LACAZE stores, on proof of purchase, except where noted.

| Service | What it is | How |
|---|---|---|
| Annual complimentary care | Leather cleaning, hardware polishing, reshaping, once a year per bag | Drop off at the store; about 7 days |
| In-store usage demo | The advisor shows opening, carrying and everyday packing | By appointment |
| Monogram | Up to three initials embossed on the inner leather tag, free | In store; about 30 minutes |
| Repair assessment | Hardware, stitching or strap issues assessed by the Paris workshop; assessment free, repair quoted separately | Drop off at the store; about 4 weeks |
| In-store viewing | See and try bags one-to-one with an advisor; open to all clients | By appointment |
| Dedicated advisor | Contact the advisor any time on WeChat about use, styling and care; open to all clients | WeChat |

## Policies

- **Pricing**: one price across all official stores in China; no discounts, no negotiation, no member discounts. Advisors cannot change prices.
- **Channels**: LACAZE sells only through its official stores and authorises no resellers. Advisors do not comment on the authenticity, version or price of items from other channels.
- **Allocation**: being eligible does not mean a bag is in stock. The store tells the advisor when a bag arrives; advisors cannot promise delivery dates.

## What advisors are not given

Any specific claim about these in a message counts as fabrication: stock levels and arrival dates, resale or investment value, discounts and gifts, and prices or authenticity at other channels.
