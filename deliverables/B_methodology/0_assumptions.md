# 0. A fictional brand

The brief gives no brand, so the first step was to create one: LACAZE. It was founded in Paris in 1923 as a workshop for travel leather goods and saddlery, and later made only handbags. Its tone is restrained, classic and craft-led: no discounts, no price comparisons, no promotions. The line has only six bags, priced from CNY 35,000 to CNY 380,000, and the two most expensive can only be bought once a client has spent enough. This mirrors what real luxury advisors face every day: the bag a client wants is not always available to them, and when a client finds it too expensive, the advisor cannot lower the price.

Clients fall into five tiers by their cumulative spend at LACAZE. The tier decides which bags a client may buy and which events they may attend, but advisors never mention it to the client:

| Tier | Cumulative spend |
|---|---|
| Unclassified | No purchase yet |
| Prospect | Below CNY 50,000 |
| Core | CNY 50,000 and above |
| VIC | CNY 250,000 and above |
| VVIP | CNY 1,000,000 and above |

Around the brand sits a small set of "store data": 10 clients and their purchases, the full specification of each bag, a few store events, and the store's services and pricing policies. The tool receives only identifiers such as a client ID and product IDs, and looks up everything else in this data itself. Every price and size in a message therefore has a source, rather than coming from the model's memory or imagination.
